How 12toons works

  1. Add audio or start with a scene. Upload voice lines when you have them, or animate without audio.
  2. Select and customise characters. Use library characters or make your own in Character Studio.
  3. Animate, preview and export. Direct the scene with AI or manual timeline controls and export your video.

Animation Studio and Character Studio

Use the Animation Studio to create scenes, edit timelines and export videos. Use Character Studio to customise characters, facial features and outfits before animating.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to draw characters?

No. Start with available characters or bring your own compatible character files.

Can I use my own voice recordings?

Yes. Upload audio files or a ZIP of numbered voice lines to animate dialogue.

Which video formats can I export?

12toons supports WebM and MP4 exports.

Where is my work saved?

Projects are saved on your device; account cloud saving requires cloud configuration.
